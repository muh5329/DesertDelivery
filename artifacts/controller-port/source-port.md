# Red Sea Baron controller port

Read-only source: `/Users/mun/Documents/Projects/Red Sea Baron/scripts/player/player_controller.gd`, `movement_config.gd`, `orbit_camera.gd`, `input_bindings.gd`, plus the dodge presentation in `player_visual.gd`. No source-project files were changed.

Imported movement values: walk 4.6 m/s, sprint 8.5 m/s, ground/air acceleration 30/9 m/s², gravity 24 m/s², jump velocity 8.4 m/s, turning response 16 and floor snap .45 m. The controller exposes source load/surface speed multipliers and movement/jump/roll counters.

Imported stamina rules: maximum 100; sprint costs 18/s, regeneration 26/s after .6 s, jump costs 12 and dodge costs 27 with .65 s regeneration delay. Exhausted sprint stays latched until Shift is released with more than 20 stamina. Grounded directional dodge locks its starting direction at 12 m/s for .48 s, including the source forward roll about its .94 m center and tucked legs. The source roll center is compensated against the courier’s .82 m pelvis so an inverted head does not inherit a lower pivot. The existing capsule continues to collide throughout the dodge; no invulnerability was added.

Imported camera/input: 6.2 m foot orbit, 64° FOV, 1.65 m pivot, 3–9 m wheel zoom in .5 m increments, exponential follow response 18 and boom recovery 9. Source pitch convention was converted to this camera's positive-down convention. Mouse sensitivity is .003 horizontal/.0025 vertical; J/L turn at 1.9 rad/s and I/K pitch at 1 rad/s. Ctrl/Q dodge is mapped only by the Foot scheme, preserving Q winch in the truck.

Deliberate integration adaptations: retain variable-height jumps, airborne momentum on stick release, swimming, exact crosshair aiming, obstruction/initial-overlap camera checks and close-camera avatar hiding. Right mouse retains aiming rather than also becoming a free-cursor orbit drag. Existing vehicle cameras and 5000 m flight ceiling remain separate. Placement cancels a roll and resets its visual rotation but preserves stamina, preventing mount/dismount from refilling it. Source fall-reset/map limits were not copied because DesertDelivery owns its own island/reset/vehicle lifecycle.

Validation: existing third-person behavioral suite passes (24 checks); the focused port suite passes 25 checks. Focused `tests/controller_port_tests.gd` covers stamina timing, exhaustion/release, costs, affordability, camera zoom/framing, command mapping, locked roll direction, 60/120 Hz roll distance and physical wall contact. `hold_controls()` cancels buffered inputs and roll presentation before Mayor freezes physics, preserving stamina; its regression also covers a grounded landing-buffered jump impulse. Independent source review accepted stamina/roll/modal logic and requested a final-partial-roll mount lifecycle check. Rider hides and disables the on-foot actor while showing a separate seated model; dismount calls place before revealing it. The additional hidden/disabled placement regression passes with neutral pose and unchanged stamina. Runtime graphical dodge inspection is coordinated by the parent agent. Tests retain the pre-existing two-ObjectDB shutdown warning; no assertions fail.
