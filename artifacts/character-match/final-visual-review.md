# Pass 11 final visual review

Reviewed `pass11.png` and `studio.png` against the supplied four-view reference. The studio image provides clearer garment and footwear inspection; the neutral pass provides continuity with earlier iterations. This review is qualitative. It does not establish an objective similarity percentage or AAA quality.

## Review boundary and independence

The world/visual reviewer critiqued the earlier full character passes and later authored the revised `character_hair.py`. Therefore the hair observations below are author verification, not independent approval; the controls agent is the independent hair critic. Other character geometry, rigging, face projection, accessories and footwear were implemented by other agents. No additional renderers or animation tests were run for this review.

## Visible blockers

The pass 10 scalp openings are resolved. No pale head surface protrudes through the crown in the supplied front, back or either profile. The enlarged coverage core remains beneath the overall layered silhouette. No new static coverage blocker was observed in pass 11 or the studio image.

Previously raised visible defects remain corrected: detached hip ledge/apron construction, nearly fused lower trouser legs, misplaced back suspender junction, short sleeves and oversized forearms, oversized head, low trouser cuffs, hidden straps, prominent collar slivers, insufficient boot length, and untapered socks. These corrections are assessed from the available views, not from all possible camera angles or animation states.

| Criterion | Assessment | Remaining difference |
|---|---|---|
| Body proportion and overall silhouette | Close to reference | Minor pose, shoulder and hip contour differences remain; no further broad proportion shift is justified by these views. |

| Color and finish | Coherent palette, simplified surface treatment | Studio lighting is lighter and softer than the reference. Hair, skin, fabric and leather have less nuanced surface definition. |
| Animation and gameplay integration | Outside this static signoff | Separate rig, mounted-pose and runtime tests must supply evidence; this report does not certify them. |

## Acceptance

Accept pass 11 as a substantial reference-directed improvement with the identified static coverage blockers resolved. Do not describe this as a demonstrated 90% match: no agreed objective scoring method exists, and visible hair, facial and fine-tailoring differences remain. It is reasonable to present the actual renders and this assessment, separating visual likeness from runtime and animation verification.

The parent reports a geometry reduction from approximately 65,000 to 49,900 authored vertices, including shirt and trouser reductions. This review did not independently measure those counts. The supplied captures show no obvious silhouette collapse or new large faceting from that reduction; they do not establish performance improvement or deformation quality.
