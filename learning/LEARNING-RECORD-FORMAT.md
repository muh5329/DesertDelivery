# Format: learning-records/NNNN-name.md

One record per non-obvious lesson learned, decision made, or misconception corrected.
Numbered, immutable-ish: supersede rather than rewrite.

```
# NNNN — <title>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** current / superseded by NNNN

## What happened
The lesson, question, or moment that produced this.

## What I now believe
Stated plainly. This is the thing to remember.

## What I got wrong first
The misconception, if there was one. This is the most valuable part —
it is what makes the correction stick.

## What this unlocks
What is now in reach that wasn't before. Feeds the next lesson's scope.
```
