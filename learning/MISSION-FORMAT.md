# Format: MISSION.md

Short. One page at most. It exists so that every lesson can be checked against it.

```
# Mission

## The want
One or two sentences, in the learner's own words where possible. Not sanitised.

## Why now
What triggered this. What is currently painful or blocked.

## What success looks like
Concrete and observable. Something you could watch the learner do.
Avoid "understand X" — prefer "can do X unaided".

## Out of scope
What we are deliberately NOT learning, so lessons don't drift.

## Signals I'm on track
Two or three things that would show real progress.
```

Revisit whenever a lesson starts to feel abstract — that is usually the mission
being wrong, not the lesson.
